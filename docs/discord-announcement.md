**📣 Riffado is getting a redesign**

Hey everyone 👋 With 26 of you here, this feels like the right size to share early and get real feedback. Here's what we're working on:

**🎨 A new look**
• Warm, calm palette: cream backgrounds with a single terracotta accent
• A dot-matrix waveform and timers, echoing the logo
• A darker "night" mode for playback and recording

**📚 A new web library**
• Sidebar with Library, Folders, and your connected recorders (Note Pro, NotePin)
• Recordings grouped by Today, Yesterday, and This week, with a transcript snippet for each
• Status dots so you always know where a recording is:
  🟢 synced to your server or cloud
  🟡 still on your phone, waiting for Wi-Fi
  ⚪ not transcribed yet

**📝 A better recording view**
• Summary up top, then key decisions with jump-to-timestamp buttons
• Tabs for Overview, Transcript, Notes, and Ask
• A floating player with skip and speed controls

**🔒 Same principle as always**
Your audio, transcripts and keys stay on your server unless you choose otherwise. The app will always show you where your data lives.

**🙋 We'd love your input**
• What annoys you most in the current UI?
• What's missing from your workflow?
Reply here or DM us. Mockups are coming to this channel soon 👀
