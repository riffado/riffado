---
title: Connect your Plaud account
description: Sign in to Plaud from Riffado, including the Google/Apple workaround.
---

Riffado signs into Plaud directly — the same way the official Plaud app
does. No screen-scraping, no headless browser, no reverse-engineered
mobile-app traffic. The flow you'll use depends on how you originally
created your Plaud account.

## Email-and-code (default path)

If you signed up to Plaud with an email address and password, you can sign
in from Riffado directly:

1. Enter the email address you use on [plaud.ai](https://plaud.ai).
2. Plaud emails you a 6-digit verification code.
3. Paste it into Riffado.

The code is forwarded to Plaud's servers and **never stored locally**.
After login, the returned access and refresh tokens are encrypted with
AES-256-GCM before they hit the database. See
[Encryption at rest](../reference/encryption-at-rest) for what that
covers.

Plaud's account region (Global, EU, Asia Pacific) is detected
automatically: if the global endpoint responds with `status: -302` and a
regional API host in `data.domains.api`, Riffado follows the redirect
and stores the per-account region alongside the token.

## Signed up with Google or Apple?

If you originally tapped **Continue with Google** or **Continue with
Apple** on Plaud, the email-and-code flow will *appear* to succeed but
sync will return zero recordings. That's because the email/password
identity and the Google/Apple identity are two different accounts on
Plaud's side, even when they share an email address
([#65](https://github.com/riffado/riffado/issues/65)).

Real "Sign in with Google / Apple" inside Riffado is structurally
blocked by Google's authorized-origins policy on Plaud's OAuth client.
There are two workarounds.

### Easy path: the connector extension

Install the [Riffado Connector](https://github.com/riffado/connector/releases/latest)
browser extension (AGPL-3.0) — grab the latest release from GitHub and
load it as an unpacked extension. With the extension installed, the
connect screen surfaces a **Sign in with Plaud** button. You sign in to
`web.plaud.ai` the way you normally do; the extension forwards the
resulting sign-in (access token and refresh token) to your Riffado
instance. No copy-pasting, no devtools.

### Manual fallback: paste the token

If you can't or won't install the extension, you can paste the access
token by hand. It lasts 24 hours, so syncing stops a day later until you
reconnect. Prefer the connector or the email code.

1. Open [web.plaud.ai](https://web.plaud.ai) in a tab and sign in with
   Google or Apple as usual.
2. Open browser devtools (F12 / Cmd+Option+I) → **Application** →
   **Cookies** → `https://web.plaud.ai`.
3. Copy the value of the `pld_ut` cookie (the long `eyJ…` JWT).
4. In Riffado, switch to the **Paste token** tab, pick the region that
   matches the API host you see in the **Network** tab (EU for
   `api-euc1.plaud.ai`, APAC for `api-apse1.plaud.ai`, otherwise
   Global), and paste.

Don't copy the `Authorization` header from a `/device/list` or
`/file/simple/web` request. That's a workspace token Riffado can't use.
The pasted token is encrypted at rest the same way as a code-flow token.

## How long does a connection last?

- **Email code:** 30 days.
- **Sign in with Plaud (connector):** 30 days with a connector version
  that forwards the refresh token. Riffado renews the 24-hour access
  token in the background until then.
- **Pasted token:** 24 hours.

Plaud's refresh tokens never extend a sign-in past its original end.
Settings → Plaud account shows when your connection ends, and the
dashboard shows a reconnect banner three days before. Repeat whichever
flow above you used originally. Tokens issued by Plaud before it added
refresh tokens last about 300 days and keep working until they expire.

## Switching accounts

You can disconnect or switch the connected Plaud account at any time
from **Settings → Plaud account**. The encrypted tokens are
deleted; previously synced recordings stay where they are (you own that
data on your own storage).
